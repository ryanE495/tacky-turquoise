Because turquoise is soft and porous, the wrong cleaner can dull or discolor it.
This post covers a safe routine plus what to keep it away from.

## The gentle routine

A soft, dry cloth handles most day-to-day cleaning. Skip ultrasonic cleaners,
harsh chemicals, and long soaks entirely.
