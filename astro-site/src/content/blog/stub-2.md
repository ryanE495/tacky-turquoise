Despite the shared name, German silver contains no silver at all. This post
explains what each metal actually is and when you'd want one over the other.

## What's in the metal

Sterling is 92.5% silver; German silver is a copper-nickel-zinc alloy. That one
fact drives most of the differences in tarnish, weight, and price.
